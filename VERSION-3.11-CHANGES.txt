VERSION 3.11 - LIGHTWEIGHT MOBILE LYRICS

- The detail page now loads albums-lite.js instead of the full lyrics database.
- Lyrics are split into one JSON file per album and loaded only for the album being viewed.
- Initial album data on the detail page was reduced from about 182 KB to about 29 KB.
- Heavy blur, shadow, and transition effects are disabled on small screens.
- Mobile lyrics use normal page scrolling instead of a nested scroll area.
